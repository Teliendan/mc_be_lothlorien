> **Test build.** This is a pre-release for testing. Things may still change before the public release, so back up any world you care about. Feedback is welcome.

Lothlórien adds a rare, peaceful forest biome: golden Mallorn trees reaching into the sky, elven villages in their crowns, gentle wildlife, and elves who remember how you treat their forest. A skin pack with six elf skins comes with it.

It works on its own and needs none of the author's other add-ons. No experimental features need to be switched on.

The biome and its buildings appear only in newly generated chunks, so start a new world or explore areas you have not visited yet.

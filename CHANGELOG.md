# Changelog

Player-facing changes of each published release, newest first.

## 0.9.0 (2026-11-22)

> Changes marked 🌱 appear only in newly generated chunks: explore new areas or start a new world.

### New

- **Mallorn Boat**: Mallorn boats rock when you hit them, just like vanilla boats.
- A new skin pack adds six elf skins for your character: Warden, Sentinel, Forester, Pathfinder, Highborn Lady and Highborn Lord.

### Changed

- **Mallorn Leaf Litter**: Adding more leaf litter or blossoms to a patch, and catching a firefly, now swing your arm in first person like any other placement. Other players now also see your arm swing when you strip a log, bottle dew or nectar, use bone meal or release a firefly.
- **Elf**: Elves and Elven Wardens now open ordinary wooden doors. Open Mallorn doors no longer block them, so they walk through; closed Mallorn doors still stop them.
- **Elf**: Elves walk around their posts again instead of standing still, and no longer climb out over fences, walls, lanterns, firefly jars or pots beside a block. Elven Wardens avoid them too.
- **Firefly**: Catching a firefly with a full inventory is now refused instead of dropping the bottle: the firefly stays free, you hear a soft "full" sound and a chat line asks you to make room.
- **Golden Mallorn Leaves**: Golden Mallorn leaves now drop sticks at the same rate as ordinary leaves.
- Mallorn Heartwood blocks, boats and the Heartwood Elven Lantern are now called Golden Mallorn (Golden Mallorn Planks, Golden Elven Lantern and so on), in every language.
- **Mallorn Leaves**: Leaves cut off from a felled Mallorn tree now fade at the same pace as oak leaves and break with their usual drops. Placing a log back into a fading crown saves the leaves within reach of it. Leaves you place yourself never fade.
- Flowers, ferns, saplings, corn, nectar blooms, leaf litter, blossoms, antlers, lanterns, buttons, pressure plates, doors, fence gates and Elven Rope no longer hold other blocks. Nothing can be placed on them or hung from them, like their vanilla counterparts.
- The packs now show as "Lothlórien" in the game's pack lists.
- Translations in all eight languages were reviewed: names of vanilla items, blocks, mobs and mechanics now match the game's own wording, and the rest reads more naturally. French now addresses the player as "vous", like the game.
- The Loremaster now stands at the reading circle. The central court also has a new resting corner with cushions and a decorated pot. 🌱
- **Harmony**: Wildlife now reacts at once when your Harmony changes, and each player's own Harmony decides whom an animal runs from. With several players nearby it judges distance by the nearest one, so it may run from a player with lower Harmony who stands farther off than usual. Animals saved in an existing world stay fearless until something alarms them.

### Fixed

- **Mallorn Boat**: Breaking a Mallorn boat with an axe now drops that Mallorn boat instead of an oak boat. Boats with a chest and the Golden Mallorn boats do the same.
- **Mallorn Boat**: Mallorn boats now sit at the vanilla height on the water, so the floor stays dry while the boat bobs. Riders sit slightly higher with it.
- **Elf**: Elves no longer get boxed in by the moss-bed borders in new villages. Home doorways and the narrow deck lanes beside the trees stay clear, so elves reach their posts and walk the whole deck. Where a moss bed would have blocked a narrow lane, single flower pots stand on the deck floor instead. 🌱
- **Mallorn Leaf Litter**: Fallen Mallorn leaves and blossoms on the forest floor now lie in mixed directions instead of all facing the same way. 🌱
- Standing in the Lothlórien forest causes fewer slowdowns and freezes, especially on tablets and other weaker devices. Plants and leaves no longer pile up background work as they grow and tick. Extending a long Elven Rope, and explosions among ropes and plants, no longer cause one long freeze either.
- **Mallorn Leaves**: Mallorn and golden Mallorn leaves no longer flicker when seen from some angles.
- Mallorn saplings and the Great Mallorn sprout can now be planted on a pale moss block, like vanilla saplings.
- **Ground Squirrel**: A squirrel now always tells you why it will not take your acorn or why its errand ended, and one that runs past the forest edge no longer gives up. A hurt squirrel takes the acorn and runs the errand.
- **Swan**: Wild swans now appear on the water surface. They no longer start out under water or beneath a bank.
- You can sleep in the beds of newly generated elven villages. Before, some said the bed was obstructed. Villages already in a world keep the old problem. 🌱
- Elven villages generate with far fewer long freezes. They now build in many small steps, and the elves and furnishings appear over about a second after the buildings. 🌱
- **Elf**: Joined villages each get their own Loremaster. When two elven villages generate merged together, each now has its own Loremaster and its own set of craft elves. 🌱
- Village harp music now fades out gently at the end of each piece instead of stopping mid-phrase.
- **White Deer**: A white deer that takes your Mallorn acorn leads you to its gift again and lays the nut at its feet when you arrive, instead of saying it has nowhere to lead you. It no longer leads to tree platforms or stands silently: a second acorn is answered, and if the walk breaks off you are told. A white deer you hurt leads again on the first acorn, and it answers when it is startled.
- **Deer**: Deer and other shy animals keep their intended distance again: a friend can walk up to about three blocks, a stranger about ten, and animals run from worse company from farther away. They still run from wolves, but zombies and other monsters no longer scare them.

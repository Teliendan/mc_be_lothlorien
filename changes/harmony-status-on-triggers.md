Your Harmony status no longer stays on screen the whole time you are in the forest. It now shows for a few seconds when it matters: when you enter the forest, when it changes or a deed costs Harmony, when an animal or an elf turns you away, and when a warden greets you or starts shooting. Elf conversation windows show it in grey below the elf's words, with your Renown.

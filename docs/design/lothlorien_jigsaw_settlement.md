# Lothlórien Elven Village (jigsaw settlement): final design, decisions, toolkit

Accepted by the owner 2026-10-09 ("I think it is finally working!"). Pieces, pools and the `/place`-only structure
`lothlorien:elven_village` are generated by `tools/build_village.mjs`. Engine facts: workspace skill
`.claude/skills/bedrock-modding/references/10-jigsaw-pieces.md`. Numbers and per-piece details: `docs/TECHNICAL_NOTES.md`, section "Elven village".
The original concept text (nodes, connector standard, pools, depth limit) was folded into this page; its step-by-step prototype plan is done and removed.

## 1. Final design

- **Jigsaw grows a tree, a runtime planner adds loops.** Worldgen (jigsaw, `max_depth` 2) builds: central tree (`central_mallorn_01`, rope to the ground, the only
  ground access) -> `combo_<node>_<face>_bridge_<L>` pieces (ONE piece = slab-arched straight bridge 7..15 + the tree it leads to, so a bridge never ends in the air) ->
  exits end in `railing_end`, `balcony_braced` (on its own log pillar) or `lookout_01`. Trees = platform piece + rollable crown piece (`crown_*`), two-level towers with a
  slab spiral stair, giant-Mallorn style trunks. Pools: `start`, `combos`, `exits`, `plugs`, `crowns`, `crowns_fallback`, `crowns_small`, `crowns_central`.
- **Connector standard** (every piece): one `minecraft:jigsaw` per connection in the deck layer on the outer face of the piece box, centre of a 3-wide walk opening,
  deck under 5 cells, rails at +-2, name/target `lothlorien:village_deck` (`village_deck_hi` = upper level, parent only), joint aligned, final state planks.
- **Standing rules** for every walk surface: light >= 8 (lanterns), headroom >= 1.9 (also over the NEXT stair tread), free ring round the trunk on stair decks,
  continuous rails (stepped on arches) that win over leaves, bridge body never one slab thin, everything supported (pillars/braces, no floating balcony), half-block steps only.
- **Loop planner** (`scripts/loop_marker.js` + `village_planner.js` + `village_loop.js`): the first `loop_marker` entity that loads starts ONE run per village: a script
  ticking area grown until no new marker appears, candidates (exit-exit, exit-side, side-side, plaza, L), scored by walking distance saved, greedy, budget 3, all written in one job.
  Merge joins (touching deck caps opened into one deck) come first, cap 6, not part of the budget. L-joins capped at total length 16 / leg 10.
- **Wardens** (`elven_warden`, village group) and `rail_mender` markers are template entities of the pieces.

## 5. Village toolkit and next steps

**How pieces are made** (`node tools/build_village.mjs`, then `.\mods verify lothlorien`; it deletes and rewrites all `structures/lothlorien/village/*`, pools and the structure, output is byte-stable):
`village_mallorn.mjs` = deck/rail/lantern/tree geometry (`Deck`, `buildTree`, `buildCrown`, `SHAPES`, `writeRails`, `addLanterns`, `RAIL`, `FLOOR_H` 16, `LEVEL_H` 8, `ROOTS` 18),
`village_combo.mjs` = bridge + node combos and `buildBalcony`, `flet_mallorn.mjs` = giant-tree shapes, `build_structures.mjs` = `.mcstructure` writer, `build_village.mjs` = pieces,
weights (`EXIT_WEIGHTS`, `EARLY_END`, `WARDENS`), pools, structure. Runtime shape shared with the generator: `scripts/village_bridge.js` (`bridgeShape`).

**Add a new piece type** (house, under-village decoration):
1. Write a builder returning `{ blocks: Map("x,y,z" -> {name, states}), size, origin, connectors }` (see `buildBalcony` for a one-connector end piece) and call `writePiece(name, ...)` in `build_village.mjs`.
   `writePiece` runs `checkConnectors` and the shared walk check (`structure_walk.mjs`: headroom, steps, unguarded edges, rail links) for every non-crown piece; fix, do not silence.
2. Connector: deck layer, outer box face, 3-wide opening, standard names above; an end piece points at pool `minecraft:empty`. Add the piece to a pool (`pool(...)` calls at the end of the builder: `exits`
   for things hanging off a platform exit, `combos` for bridge + tree). For things attached elsewhere (e.g. under a deck) add a new pool and a jigsaw in the platform piece whose `target_pool` names it.
   The box is the collision shape, nothing may stick out; projection is `rigid`.
3. Rules every piece must pass: block light >= 8 on every walk cell (`.claude/skills/bedrock-modding/scripts/structure_light.mjs`, lanterns via `addLanterns`; tests re-check all shipped pieces);
   walkability via `structure_walk.mjs`; everything supported or visibly braced to the ground (pillar down to ROOTS), no floating parts; rails are `RAIL` posts with `minecraft:connection_*` states and win over
   leaves (clear leaves in rail/walk/headroom cells; the orphan-leaf trim is the last step); only vanilla-namespace states turn with the piece (`minecraft:block_face`, `minecraft:connection_*`),
   `lothlorien:*` states do not.
4. **Elf posts (M1, 2026-10-10):** a new TREE piece (anything that becomes a profession tree) needs exactly one `lothlorien:elf_post` with kind `home` (`postCells` in `build_village.mjs` does it for every `NODES` entry and so for every combo;
   central trees: 1 `loremaster` + `RESERVE_POSTS` `reserve`), or the elves never stand there: the planner run (`elf_village.js`) assigns professions only to posts it finds. Pieces that are not trees (crowns, ends) carry none.
   Post rules and tunables live in `scripts/elf_rules.js`; tests check count, standing cells and spacing for every shipped piece. A piece that cannot fit a post makes the generator throw.
5. Template-entity trap: mobs saved in a `.mcstructure` load with engine-default attributes, so they need replacement on load (see `warden_replace.js`); markers (`rail_mender`, `loop_marker`) are
   placed via `extra.markers` / `extra.loopMarkers` of `writePiece`. A new platform needs `loop_marker`s on its rim runs (`sideMarkerCells`) or the planner cannot see it.
6. Add tests in `tests/run.mjs` (connector standard, no orphan leaves, light and walk sweeps run over every shipped piece automatically) and run `node tools/village_sim.mjs 300 1` (must stay at 0 failures).

**Preview tools** (run from `mods/lothlorien`; PNGs go to `temp/village_render/` and `temp/village_sim/`):
- `node tools/village_render.mjs piece <name> [rot] [--leaves]` isometric + top view of one piece; `side <piece>`; `sim <seed> [--links] [--iso x0 x1 z0 z1]` simulated village (+ planner links);
  `dump <dump.json | tests/fixtures/village_v2.json.gz> [--markers m.json] [--budget 3] [--iso]` a REAL village with the planner's picks (colours, ends, leftover candidates). No arguments = usage.
- **Textured preview** (judge colour, texture, composition without the game): add `--tex` to `piece`, `sim ... --iso` or `dump --iso` (and `--night` for lamp light, `--ppb N` pixels per block, default 16, `--rot R` view turned R quarter turns,
  `--noleaves`); `node tools/village_render.mjs mcs <any.mcstructure>` renders any structure file (e.g. a mockup in `temp/`). Scripts call `renderBlocks(Map("x,y,z" -> {name, states}), out.png, opts)` from `tools/textured_render.mjs`.
  Vanilla textures are downloaded once from Mojang's bedrock-samples into `temp/texture_cache/` (mod blocks read `lothlorien_rp`); shapes are approximations (stairs = slab + step, plants = crossed planes, custom-geometry blocks = small boxes).
- `node tools/piece_ascii.mjs <piece> [y <y> | x <z> [y0 y1] | z <x>]` ASCII layers and cuts, jigsaw list, entities; no argument lists the pieces.
- `node tools/village_sim.mjs [seeds] [first]` the assembly simulator + planner + all checks (300 seeds take about 80 s). The reusable mocked `@minecraft/server` is in
  `.claude/skills/bedrock-scripting/scripts/` (see `tests/script_glue.mjs`).

**Test in game:** `/place structure lothlorien:elven_village` (deploy first, leave and re-enter the world; worldgen changes need a NEW village). `/scriptevent lothlorien:debug` turns the debug log on (planner stages, summaries and per-link lines then appear in the content log; failures always log).
Content log lines (debug mode): `[lothlorien] planner: ticking area quota maxChunkCount=N`, `planner: start for village near x,z`, `planner: village at x,z: N markers ... link(s)`.
Inspect a saved world without the game: `python -B tools/world_scan.py "<world>" --dump X0 Y0 Z0 X1 Y1 Z1 out.json` (then `village_render.mjs dump`), and
`python -B tools/world_scan.py "<world>" --actors lothlorien:loop_marker --json m.json` (marker positions for the planner; without `--json` it prints mob attributes).

**Planner facts to remember:** budget 3 links (`PLAN.budget`), merges cap 6 outside the budget, L cap total 16 / leg 10 (`lMaxLen`, `lMaxLeg`), a link must save >= 30 walking steps and be 2.5x shorter,
join kinds exit / side / sideside / plaza / L / merge, clutter rule (no new join within 2 cells of another deck), ticking-area quota 300 chunks (verified), area margins 32/28/24, seed 48.
Constants: `village_planner.js` (`PLAN`, `AREA`), `village_loop.js` (`LOOP`, `PLAZA`, `MERGE`).

fallbacks and intrinsic leaf caps below deck +6 replace the former bare-stub
outcome when a neighboring box refuses a larger crown. Balcony/lookout pillars

root flares and forest-floor dressing, completes borders and closes unused exits.

removed after acceptance, while the production generators and simulator remain.
